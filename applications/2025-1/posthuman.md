# POSTHUMAN Validator Introduction
![banner](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/fad5dd87-b320-4ade-8c9b-63d12a792666)

**POSTHUMAN** is a community-driven, decentralized validator active since 2019, securing and supporting Proof-of-Stake networks with a strong combination of technical excellence and ecosystem engagement.

Our validator is governed by delegators through **DAS (Decentralized Autonomous Synchronization)** — a decentralized governance system deployed on **Juno Network** via [DaoDao](https://daodao.zone/dao/juno1h5ex5dn62arjwvwkh88r475dap8qppmmec4sgxzmtdn5tnmke3lqwpplgg). Every delegator can directly participate in decision-making, setting us apart from traditional centralized validators.

Our team consists of 16 experienced contributors specializing in infrastructure, development, education, content creation, and community building. We validate over **40+ networks**, maintain high-availability infrastructure, run public RPC services, and actively contribute to the growth of each ecosystem we support.

Our objectives are:
- **Strengthening decentralized ecosystems** through reliable validation and infrastructure services ([Explore our infrastructure services](https://nodes.posthuman.digital/))
- **Developing communities** by running educational initiatives like Validator School and Cosmos Academy
- **Supporting decentralized governance research** and driving new governance models ([Research](https://github.com/Antropocosmist/research))
- **Growing Web3 ecosystems** with active involvement, contributions, and public advocacy

We also maintain strong visibility across platforms, operating multiple major community channels:
- [Cosmos Ecosystem X](https://x.com/CosmosEcosystem) – 34.8k followers
- [POSTHUMAN X](https://x.com/POSTHUMAN_DVS) – 7.5k followers
- [CryptoBase YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@CRYPTOBASED) – 12.2k subscribers
- [Russian-speaking Cosmos Ecosystem Telegram](https://t.me/CosmosEcosystem_ru) – ~8k members
- [Russian-speaking Juno Community Telegram](https://t.me/juno_ru)
- [English-speaking POSTHUMAN Community Telegram](https://t.me/posthumanchat) – 1.2k members

Since the launch of the Juno Network, we have been actively developing the **[Russian-speaking Juno community](https://t.me/juno_ru)** and have actively used and promoted everything built on top of Juno.

At POSTHUMAN, we work transparently, decentralize power to our delegators, and aim to drive long-term sustainability, education, and innovation across all networks we validate.

More information: [posthuman.digital](https://posthuman.digital)
